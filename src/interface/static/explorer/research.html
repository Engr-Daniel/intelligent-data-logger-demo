INTERPRETATION

Correctness needs coverage

Recent-only views can resolve acute alarms, but abstain when a historical comparison is unavailable. An unanswered identifiable case counts as unresolved in the primary outcome. High selective accuracy can coexist with low coverage.

Intervals resample whole installations (2,000 bootstrap draws). Zero-width intervals mean identical scores across these simulated installations; they do not establish certain field performance.

FAILURES REMAIN VISIBLE

Seven unresolved or incorrect clean cases

Full access missed five gradual declines, labelled one normal day as weather loss, and abstained on one normal day. Injection labels and engineering descriptions are not always equivalent: natural weather variation can occur on a control day.

Shared simulator/policy assumptions, authored task mix and limited corruption models constrain generalization. Independent data and expert assessment remain future work.
