# Stage 5: Screen of initiatives, Petgrave.io IDL edition

Run with `blueprint-screen` on 17 Oct 2026. The tests:

1. grounded in the business model;
2. serves a KPI and a decision;
3. fits maturity (no pillar more than one level above today);
4. closes a real gap;
5. has a named customer;
6. stays inside the vision;
7. can be explained without naming AI or a tool. This test is in the Blueprint's Appendix C.

> **The verdict that should surprise us.** The most valuable next step for an AI data logger involves no AI: write down what the installer found (I01). The most exciting proposals are all sound, but early. Our own hardware, other energy systems, forecasting and carbon credits each need field data that does not exist yet.
