# Stage 5: Value Proposition. I01, record what installers find on call-backs

Status: ◐ provisional · Last updated: 17 Oct 2026 · Closes G02 · Metric: M01

## Customer profile

**Customer:** the installer who goes back to a customer's site. The research team is a second customer: it needs the installer's finding as the reference label.

| Jobs (decisions they own) | Pains | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| Decide whether a complaint needs a visit · find the fault on site · settle "your equipment or your load?" · decide whether to charge for the visit | Return visits are common (6 of 7 had 1–2 a year, C16). The customer often noticed late, when the power went off (C15). Without a record, it's word against word. | Fewer wasted trips · arriving knowing what's wrong · a record that shows the fault wasn't their workmanship · evidence for the next customer |

## Value map

**The initiative, described as an outcome:** *every return visit leaves a two-line record of what was wrong and how long it had been wrong.*

- **Pain relievers:**
  - a WhatsApp-sized form (what was wrong · how it was found · when it probably started · what was done), so it costs one minute;
  - the record is the installer's own, kept with the site.
- **Gain creators:**
  - with the record, the IDL's diagnosis for that site can be checked (M01);
  - over time, the installer can show a stranger "faults found and fixed" instead of asking them to take it on trust.

## Five safety checks

| Check | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can the value be explained without mentioning AI or tools? | Yes: "write down what you found". |
| Is there a clear decision owner? | Yes: the installer for the visit; Daniel for M01. |
| Does it fit current maturity? | Yes: it needs Processes at Level 2 at most. |
| Would the customer notice if it did not exist? | The research would: without it, M01 cannot be measured. Installers may not at first, so the value exchange must be shown back to them (see `links.md`). |
| Is the solution proportional? | Yes: a form, not a platform. |

**Verdict: Proceed.** Start with the installer pack (`../../instruments/pack-installers-2026-10-17.md`). Ask for their last three call-backs first, then a standing log during the pilot.

**What would change this verdict:** if fewer than 3 installers agree to log findings (M07), use owner-reported outcomes instead. Those are weaker labels, and the change would be stated in the paper.

**The question this raises next.** What future are we moving toward if this and the other initiatives work?
