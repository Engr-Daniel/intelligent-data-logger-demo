## The statement

> **Plain version.** We aim to show that a solar data logger can answer an owner's questions with evidence a stranger can check, by running our method on a few real installations whose installers record what they actually found, so that problems are fixed before the lights go off and the savings are there to see, without claiming field accuracy before we have measured it, letting the AI make the engineering call, or building hardware before the data we already have has been tried.

> **For the team wall.** *No answer without evidence; no claim without a field check.*
