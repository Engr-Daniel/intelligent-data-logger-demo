# Stage 3: Data Maturity Assessment. System-owner edition

Status: ◐ provisional · Last updated: 17 Oct 2026 · Scored for the segment "organisations running solar + grid + generator without monitoring", from one reported case (C14) and installer reports (C15–C17). Check with real owners.

| Pillar | Score | Evidence | Constraint to the next level | Next target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| People & Culture | 2 | Management wants ROI and contribution figures; it depends on one person who can produce them (C14) | Interest without capability | **3:** the monthly energy numbers are discussed at a management meeting as a matter of routine |
| Processes | **1** | Readings are taken by hand, when someone remembers; analysis is manual and occasional (C14). Check-ups are rare (C15). | Everything rests on one person's time | **2:** one complete record every week without anyone writing it down |
| Technology | 1–2 | The inverter records data, but it stays on the device screen or a vendor app; most systems have no monitoring (C17) | Data never leaves the device | **2:** readings leave the device every week (photo, export or app) |
| Governance | 1 | No agreed definition of "solar contribution" or "savings"; nobody is named as the owner of the energy record | Each report can count differently | **2:** one written definition of savings, agreed with management |
| Strategy & Impact | 1 | Energy decisions (fuel, repairs, expansion) are made when something breaks | Decisions come before the data | **2:** one decision a month made from the numbers |

**Limiting constraint: Processes.** The record depends on a person writing it down. That is exactly the work the IDL is designed to take over.

**Not yet feasible for this segment:**
- real-time alerts (no connectivity);
- automated control of loads or the generator;
- any comparison across sites.

**Failure patterns present:** no ownership (Governance 1). "Dashboards = maturity" is a risk if owners think the vendor app is the record.
