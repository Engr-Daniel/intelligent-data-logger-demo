# Stage 5: Value Proposition. I07, a monthly energy statement nobody has to write

Status: ◐ provisional · Last updated: 17 Oct 2026 · Closes G11–G13 · Metrics: O1, O2, O3, O6, O7

## Customer profile

**Customer:** the person responsible for an organisation's energy, and the manager who reads their report. The worked case is the radio station's energy manager (C14).

| Jobs | Pains | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| Show management what solar saved and how much of the power it supplied · keep the station on air · know when to call the installer | Hours of reading, writing and re-typing · numbers that depend on unwritten assumptions · faults found only when the power fails | A report that is ready on the first of the month · an early warning · answers to "why?" without a spreadsheet |

## Value map

**The initiative, described as an outcome:** *on the first of every month, the energy manager has the statement they used to spend evenings preparing. It shows what the system made, what it saved against grid and generator, the fuel and CO₂ avoided, anything unusual, and what every number assumed. They can ask follow-up questions in plain language.*

- **Pain relievers:**
  - no manual capture or re-typing (the logger's record);
  - every figure shows its assumptions (the receipt);
  - the gap in the record is shown, not guessed.
- **Gain creators:**
  - O1 warns about decline before it becomes a failure;
  - O2 is split by what solar replaced, so the saving can be defended to management.

## Five safety checks

| Check | Answer |
|---|---|
| Explained without naming AI or a tool? | Yes: "your monthly energy report, written for you" |
| Clear decision owner? | Yes: the energy manager |
| Fits maturity? | Yes, with a weekly data route (Processes 1 → 2). Real-time is not needed. |
| Would they notice if it were gone? | Yes. Without it, they go back to the notebook (C14). |
| Proportional? | Yes. A monthly statement, not a control system. |

**Verdict: Proceed in the pilot (I04).** Produce the statement for each pilot site from month one.

**What would change this verdict:** owners who don't read it. Track whether the statement is opened and whether any decision follows (petgrave-idl RQ4).
