# Stage 1: Business Model Mapping. System-owner edition

*How does energy really work for an organisation that runs solar, grid and a generator?* Status: ◐ provisional · Last updated: 17 Oct 2026

**The worked case** is a radio station where Daniel managed the energy: solar, grid and generator (C14). It is one reported case, so read the canvas as a pattern to check with 2–3 other owners, not as a fact about all owners. The station is not named.

> **The thing to see first.** For a radio station, the product is time on air. Energy is not a bill to cut. It is the thing that keeps the business running. Yet the record of that energy was a notebook, and the analysis was one person's evenings. When that person is busy or leaves, the organisation loses its memory of how its power behaves.

| Block | As-is today, in the owner's terms | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Who depends on the energy** | Listeners and advertisers (on-air time) · staff · management, who need the numbers | C14 |
| **What the energy must deliver** | No dead air. Power for transmitters and studios through grid outages. | C14 (reported) |
| **How energy arrives** | Solar with a battery · the grid when it is on · a generator as backup | C14 |
| **Relationship with the installer** | They are called when something fails. Maintenance and check-ups are rare (C15). Return visits are common for installers (6 of 7 had 1–2 a year, C16). | C15, C16 |
| **Where the money goes and comes back** | Capital spent on solar · fuel and generator servicing · grid bills. "Savings" is what is no longer spent on fuel and grid. | C09, C20 |
| **Key resources** | The equipment · **the person who keeps the records** · the logbook or spreadsheet | C14 |
| **Key activities** | Reading the inverter, meter and generator hours · writing them down · working out solar contribution, consumption and ROI · reporting to management · calling the installer | C14 |
| **Key partners** | The installer · fuel suppliers · the electricity distribution company | C14 |
| **Costs that matter** | Fuel (price moves monthly) · generator wear · downtime · staff time spent recording and analysing | S12, C14 |
