# Stage 6: Data Vision. System-owner edition

Status: ◐ provisional · Last updated: 17 Oct 2026

| Component | Draft |
|---|---|
| Capability shift | From readings written by hand when someone remembers (Processes 1) to a complete weekly record nobody has to write (Processes 2) |
| Decision change | We call the installer when the numbers say the system is slipping, not when the power goes off. We report savings that management can check. |
| Business value | Fewer outages, fuel spending we can see shrinking, and a defensible ROI and sustainability record |
| Boundaries | We won't trust a number whose assumptions we can't see · we won't let the system switch our equipment by itself · we won't share our usage data without saying yes first |

## The statement

> **Plain version.** We aim to know every month whether our solar is working, what it saved us and what it kept out of the air, by letting the logger keep the record we used to write by hand, so that we fix problems before they take us off air and can show management the numbers, without trusting any figure whose assumptions we can't see or sharing our data without our say.

> **In an energy manager's words.** *"I used to spend my evenings with the logbook. Now I read the statement, ask why if something looks odd, and call the installer before the power goes."*

## Validation checks

- ✓ understandable;
- ✓ achievable (weekly data, not real-time);
- ✓ a filter: it rules out automatic control and unexplained numbers;
- ✓ value-linked;
- ✓ human-centred.
