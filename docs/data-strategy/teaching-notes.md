# Teaching the Blueprint with the Intelligent Data Logger

*For Petgrave.io workshops with business owners and leaders after NASEF 2026 · Draft, 17 Oct 2026*

The Blueprint guide uses Meridian Distribution, an invented logistics firm, as its worked example. This engagement is a **real** one, and most business owners have lived its central case: someone in the organisation keeps the energy records by hand. Use the **system-owner edition** as the main example. Use the **Petgrave.io edition** to show leaders how the same method disciplines an innovation project.

## The arc of a half-day workshop

| Stage | The question | The IDL moment to show | Exercise for participants (10–15 min) | The lesson to land |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Business model | How does it really work? | For a radio station, energy is time on air, not just a bill (system-owner, Stage 1) | Map your own critical resource the same way: what breaks first if it fails? | Describe the business as it is, not as the pitch says |
| 2 · KPIs | How do we know it's working? | "Total kWh generated" is a vanity metric; "money saved, split by what solar replaced" changes a decision | Take three numbers you report. For each one, ask "what would we do differently if this changed?" | No decision, no metric |
| 3 · Maturity | What are we capable of? | The record depended on one person's evenings: Processes at Level 1 | Score your five pillars privately, then compare scores. The spread is the finding. | Your lowest pillar sets the ceiling |
| 4 · Lifecycle | Where is value lost? | The logbook: capture by hand, act after the failure (`logbook.png`) | Walk one recent incident from capture to action. Where did someone wait, re-type or guess? | Fix the breaks that block decisions |
| 5 · Value | Who benefits, and how? | "A monthly energy report nobody has to write": the outcome stated without naming AI | Rewrite one proposed tool purchase as an outcome for a named person | If you can't explain it without the tool's name, it isn't ready |
| 6 · Vision | Where are we heading? | Boundaries: no numbers without visible assumptions; no automatic switching | Write your vision with a "we will not" line | A vision without boundaries is a wish |

## Two reveals that work in a room

1. **"What did solar save you?"** Ask the room to guess. Then show `payback.png`: 5.1 years or 0.9 years from the same readings. The difference is one question nobody asked: was the grid on? *Lesson: the definition of a metric is a strategic decision.*
2. **"Should an AI company build its own hardware first?"** Show the Petgrave.io edition's verdicts (`verdicts.png`). The first step for an AI data logger involves no AI: write down what the installer found. *Lesson: maturity before complexity.*
