Hello, this is Daniel from Petgrave.io. Thank you again for your help with our installer survey.

We are testing a tool that reads a solar system's data and tells the owner or installer what is wrong, and why. Your answers decide **what it must be able to spot first**. You can skip any question, and "I don't know" is a useful answer. If you do skip one, tell us who would know.

1. **Your last three call-backs** (return visits after installation), most recent first. For each: *what was wrong · how the customer noticed · roughly how long it had been wrong before they called.*
2. On those visits, **did anyone have readings or a record** that helped? *(inverter screen / app / customer's notes / nothing)*
3. Of your customers, **how many check their system or ask for maintenance** before something fails? *(a number out of your last 10 jobs is perfect)*
4. **Which inverter brands** do you install most? Do those customers have Wi-Fi or app monitoring? *(brand: yes/no)*
5. Would you **write two lines after each call-back** (what was wrong, how found, when it started) if, in return, you got a diagnosis before you go? *(yes / maybe / no)*
6. Do you have **one or two customers** who might let us read their system weekly for a month? They would get a free monthly energy statement. *(yes, how many / no)*
7. If you don't know an answer, **who would?**

Thank you. We'll share what we learn from everyone, without naming anyone.
